# Irelia Companion 0.9.2

**The Data tab's model switches respond at once:** **Patch memory**, **Runes against the draft** and **Follow the evaluation** now show your choice as soon as you click, and the model follows with a single rebuild. The model, the builds and the evaluation are unchanged.

## What went wrong
Reproduced on a 54,000-game test database by clicking the switches in the dashboard:
- **A click during a model rebuild snapped back:** rebuilding the model takes 35–45 s at that size (every few minutes while new games arrive, and after each switch). A switch moved meanwhile was saved, but the model's part of the app only read it once the rebuild ended. The switch waited 4 seconds for an answer, then showed the old state again, then the new one 30–40 s later.
- **Clicking again made it worse:**
  - A second click on a switch that had snapped back asked for the same thing again.
  - Each click queued its own full rebuild: four clicks took four rebuilds, about 80 s.
  - **Follow the evaluation** showed on, then off, then on again: each queued click re-saved its own choice when its turn came, over the later ones.
- **A switch moved during a rebuild could be missed:** if champ select or a game started right after, the model could keep the old setting until the next new games arrived.

## What changed
- **The switch shows your choice at once and stays.** The model follows with a rebuild. If one is already running, it follows after that one ends.
- **Under the switch**, until the model has your choice:
  - "Updating the model…" while it rebuilds;
  - "The model takes this at its next update, never during champ select or a game" while it waits.
- **Several clicks, one rebuild:** switches moved together take one rebuild, and none if they cancel out. Only the app's main part saves your choices, so a later click is never overwritten by an earlier one.
- **Every rebuild reads the switches once, as it starts:** a switch moved during a rebuild always gets a rebuild of its own.

## Tested
- **On the test database, in the dashboard:**
  - Before: five clicks during a rebuild snapped back each time and queued four rebuilds.
  - Now: every click showed at once and stayed, and the model rebuilt once more after the running rebuild.
  - A single click with nothing running rebuilt at once, as before.
- **New test:** a switch moved during a build makes the next build due (this test fails on 0.9.1).
