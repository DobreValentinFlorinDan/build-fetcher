# Build Fetcher 1.5.0

**1.5.0 turns the in-game overlay into a build order: starting items first, then each next buy in order until your
build is full. It updates as the game goes, from your gold, the components you already bought, and what the enemy
team and your lane opponent build.**

## New
- Everything in 1.4.2, which was merged but not released on its own (see its notes).
- **The overlay shows a build order.** Before 1.5.0 it showed three "next buys to consider" with no order. Now it
  shows:
  - **Start with:** the starting items, during the first two minutes and only until you buy something.
  - **Built:** the items you already finished, for example "Built 3/6".
  - **Build order from here:** every remaining item in buying order, numbered up to slot 6. The first step shows
    whether you can buy it now or which part to buy with your current gold, plus two other items that fit that slot.
    The later steps show what they cost from here, and "Affordable after the above" when your gold covers them too.
- **It follows the game.** The order is worked out again with every update:
  - It follows the core build path (the first three items) that best matches what you already built, then the
    strongest next items. Boots come after your first item.
  - **Components you bought:** a part that pays for at least 40% of a planned item (or 1,000 gold of it) moves that
    item to the front, and its price counts the part as paid. A big part (800+ gold) that no planned item uses brings
    in the best item built from it.
  - **Enemy team:** if most of the enemy's damage is magic or physical, if they build healing, or if they stack
    armor or magic resist, an item that answers it moves in as your 3rd item, unless your build or boots already
    answer it. It's marked "answer" and says why.
  - **Lane opponent:** their own items count too, components included. For example, a Darius with two attack damage
    parts puts armor in as your 2nd buy (or picks Plated Steelcaps as your boots), with the reason
    "Lane opponent Darius builds attack damage".
- "Answers to the enemy build" now lists only answers the build order doesn't already include.

## Measured
- 177 Rust tests and 8 web tests pass; the web type check and build pass.
- New test: the build order at 0:20 (starting items, six slots, boots second, 500 gold priced against the first step
  only); at 25:00 against a mostly magic enemy (owned items out, magic resist next); in lane against Darius with two
  attack damage parts (armor by the 2nd buy); and with a component already bought (its item moves first, the part
  counted as paid).
