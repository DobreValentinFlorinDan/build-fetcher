# Build Fetcher 1.4.2

**1.4.2 stops the fetcher from spending requests on off-meta picks such as Olaf support, and fixes the lane check
that threw away most games with a timeline (why Fetching could show 0 matches / hour while it kept sending
requests).**

The installer is per user, with no admin rights. It installs over any earlier version and keeps your settings, key,
games and model in `%APPDATA%\build-fetcher\`. It isn't code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen warns on first run:
choose **More info → Run anyway**.

## New
- **Off-meta picks aren't hunted.** The fetcher used to look for the champion-roles with the fewest games first, so
  a troll pick with 1 game (Olaf support) came before every real pick that needed more games.
  - **Now:** a champion-role picked in under 0.5% of the patch's games is skipped when the fetcher looks for thin
    pairs. Its games still count whenever they turn up. Pick rates are re-measured on every plan, so a pick that
    becomes popular comes back on its own. Early in a patch (under 500 games) the previous patch's pick rates are
    used. Your tier A champions are never skipped.
  - The Fetching page lists the picks it is skipping.

## Fixed
- **0 matches / hour while requests kept going.** Every game with one of your tier A champions gets its timeline,
  and the timeline was used to check roles: each of the ten players had to spend half of minutes 2-10 in their role's
  area. Junglers clear camps beside the lanes and gank, and supports ward the river and roam, so most of these games
  were dropped (the "roles" count on the Fetching page).
  - **Now:** top, mid and bot laners must still be in their lane half of that time. A jungler or support is dropped
    only for spending most of it in one lane that isn't theirs, which is what a real role swap looks like.

## Measured
- Rust tests and the web type check pass.
- New tests: an off-meta pair is skipped once the patch has enough games, hunted while it is too early to tell, and
  hunted again when its pick rate is above the bar; tier A is never skipped. A jungler who clears and ganks and a
  support who roams keep their game (this fails on 1.4.1); a support on the mid lane and a roaming mid laner are still
  dropped.
