## What went wrong
- **The cause:** in the desktop app, all of the app's threads share one 4 GB of script memory. That covers its server, the model, fetching and every evaluation thread together. 0.12.0 chose how many threads to run from your computer's free memory alone. On a large database, several threads together filled those 4 GB, and the evaluation stopped.
- **How many threads now:** Evaluate builds also keeps the threads within that shared memory, leaving 1 GB for the rest of the app and room for its own first thread. On a large database that means fewer threads than your free memory alone would allow. The Data tab says so: "the app's 4 GB of script memory, shared by all its threads, holds N".
- **Each extra thread is capped:** its memory is limited to half again what it should need. One that goes past that stops on its own, and its games are worked out by the first thread, so the evaluation finishes with the same results.
