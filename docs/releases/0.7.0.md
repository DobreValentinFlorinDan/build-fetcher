# Irelia Companion 0.7.0

Stage C: the build goes into your League client with one click in champ select. Before the game, the app shows how the matchup tends to go. In game, the overlay says what to buy with the gold you have, and who is ahead.

The installer is per-user (no admin rights), lets you choose the install folder, and creates **Irelia Companion** shortcuts in the Start Menu and on the desktop. It isn't code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen will warn on first run: choose **More info → Run anyway**. Installing over 0.6.0 keeps your settings, API key and match data in `%APPDATA%\irelia-companion\`.

## What's new
- **Save runes and items to the client** (Build tab, in champ select).
  - One click saves the build you see as the **Irelia Companion** rune page, made your current page.
  - It also saves the **Irelia Companion · Top vs Darius** item set: start, core and boots in order, the three best 4th / 5th / 6th items, wards and potions. The set shows in the in-game shop.
  - It works only when you click, only in champ select, never in game.
  - It replaces only the page and set it made itself. If all your rune pages are in use, it tells you and leaves them alone; the item set is still saved.
- **Matchup facts before the game.**
  - In champ select, the Build tab shows how the matchup tends to go in stored high-elo games: Irelia's gold against this opponent at 10 and 15 minutes, and her win rate by game length, with the number of games behind each.
  - The overlay shows the same in one line during champ select.
  - Once the game starts, the Live Game card takes its place.
- **Best buy for your gold** (overlay and Live Game card).
  - Example: "With 1,250g: Sheen + Long Sword".
  - It aims at the next item: the adjusted "Next" in draft-adjusted mode, else the build plan's next item.
  - With enough gold it is the item itself. Otherwise it is the parts high-elo Irelias in your lane buy first, with games against your opponent counting most, and then the next parts the rest of your gold covers.
  - It covers your first back too. It is shop advice only: never when to go back or what to do.
- **Who is ahead** (overlay "Game" section and Live Game card).
  - Your team's item value and kill difference against the enemy team.
  - The most fed enemy (AP or AD) and the most fed ally, counted only when their items are worth well above their team's average.
  - Everything comes from the scoreboard (the items everyone can see on Tab) and is labelled as an estimate.
- **Clearer evaluation status.** "The last evaluation found no clear gain over the lane builds, so the lane build stays the default. You can still choose Draft-adjusted." The Data tab and the tooltip say what "clear" means.

## Fixes
- **Saving to the client:**
  - It saves only the build made for the current pick. A save pressed while the build was still updating could store the previous matchup's runes under the new title.
  - It requires Irelia: the rune page would otherwise become your current page on another champion.
  - The button waits while the build updates.
- **Unstable Live Client:**
  - One slow or dropped answer from the game no longer ends the game in the app; three in a row do.
  - The lane or opponent you picked survives a reconnect.
- **"All matchups" in game** keeps the CS, vision and gold comparison with the enemy in your lane.
- **Lane gold history** starts again when your lane opponent changes, instead of mixing two opponents.
- **New patch:** the build follows a new patch as soon as the app has loaded it. Before, it could keep the old patch's items until something else changed, and the best buy used the old patch's recipes and prices.
- **Background thread restarts:** a thread that keeps stopping right after it starts waits longer each time instead of restarting every second.
- **Draft model:**
  - A failed update shows its error on the Data tab instead of a silent "Ready".
  - A build that failed for any reason is retried.
- **Best buy:** the "X% of N games" text counts games. Games against your opponent still weigh more in the choice, and the text says how many there are.
- **Compacting the database** pauses the app like a data-folder move, so no request reaches a paused thread.

## After installing
Nothing to do. In your next champ select, press **Save runes and items to the client** on the Build tab once you are happy with the build. The first time, the app needs a free rune page slot.
