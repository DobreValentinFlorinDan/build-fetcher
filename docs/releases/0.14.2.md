## Opens again after an unclean exit
- **The problem:** when the app's own process ended without ending its middleware (ended by hand, or a crash), that middleware kept running. It kept the port and the database, and every later start stopped at once (`EADDRINUSE` and `EPERM … irelia.db-wal` in `logs\middleware.log`). Until you ended it in Task Manager, the app wouldn't open.
- **Ended at the next start.** The middleware's PID is kept next to the settings (`middleware.pid`) while it runs. At the next start, the app ends a middleware left behind by that PID, after checking that the PID still belongs to the app's own executable, and says so in the log.
- **Ends with the app.** The middleware checks every 5 s that the app is still there. If the app is gone, the middleware ends itself.
- **Slim stored data cut short this way is rolled back** at that start, as before: the database is checked by SHA-256 and the cache entries are put back. Nothing was replaced while the other process held the files.
- **The port fallback works.** A start that found its port taken by another program meant to use any free port, but it stopped with an unhandled error instead.

## Slim stored data shows how far it is
- **Before:** the card said "estimating the time left…" for the whole run, so a long run looked stuck.
- **Now:** each step shows its phases and what they count:
  - Step 1, the backup: the database copy in MB, its SHA-256 check, and the cache entries moved into the backup.
  - Steps 2 and 4, the results before and after: the quick evaluation's steps, building the model, and the 200 drafts.
  - Step 3, the slimming: the games, then the archived games, then freeing the disk space. That last part can't be counted, and the card says so.
- **Each phase shows its time left, as an estimate,** from its own pace. The check's phases start from what the same phase of the baseline took. The run's total time shows below.
