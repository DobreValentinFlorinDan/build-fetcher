## Late-game swaps
The Items view's **Sixth Item** showed what games finished sixth, usually after selling boots or another item, so it read like a seventh slot. 0.17.0 measures what to sell for what instead:

- **The situations:** every shop visit from 20:00 with all six slots full (five legendaries and boots, or six legendaries), and what happened to each item held: kept, sold for the next legendary finished within 10 minutes, or sold for nothing. A game's visits together count as one game.
- **How each is judged:** the result against the game's win chance just before the visit, compared with players in the same spot who kept the item. Raw win rates would mislead: games with a late sell won 42.5 % against 53 % overall, because swaps happen in long games and games going badly.
- **The advice:** for each item of the draft-adjusted build, keep it, or sell it for another only when that clearly did better than keeping in drafts like this one.
- **The check:** Evaluate builds tests whether held-out games that followed the advice did better than keeping every item. Only then does the Items view show it in place of the Sixth Item. A new **Late-game swaps** switch in the Data tab can turn it on or off yourself.

**What it found on your games** (82,069 games, 68,215 situations in 5,203 of them): keeping an item did about **7.2 points better** than selling it, even after allowing for the game state. Advice that sold in half the situations did 7.21 pts worse than keeping every item (95 % interval −9.29 to −4.90). Part of that is likely what the game state can't show, such as selling Guardian Angel right after dying with it. Since then the advice suggests a swap only when its lead over keeping is clear. That rule hasn't been measured on your games yet: your next evaluation will. Until a swap clearly beats keeping, the Items view keeps the Sixth Item.
