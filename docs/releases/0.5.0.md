# Irelia Companion 0.5.0

Draft-adjusted builds: every rune, item and skill choice is scored on high-elo games with drafts like yours, adjusted for how the game was going when it was made. You also get a review of your own games and an in-game "Next" item that follows the game. The new builds stay off until they prove better than today's lane builds on your data.

## What's new
- **Draft-adjusted builds** (Draft dashboard → **Draft-adjusted / Lane baseline**).
  - **How picks are scored.** Each decision is scored on its own: keystone, each rune row, secondary runes, shards, summoners, start, items 1-6, boots and skills. The score is how much more often than the game state predicted high-elo games like yours were won.
  - **Which games count.** Games in your lane with similar enemy and ally comps and the same lane opponent count most. Each player's games are capped at 5 % of the total, and the previous patch counts less when it changed Irelia or the item involved.
  - **Why it changed.** Every changed pick shows a reason ("enemy magic damage 62 %") and how much evidence stands behind it.
  - **Thin evidence.** A pick with too few similar games keeps today's lane build, and says so.
  - **Regions.** You can limit a draft to some regions; your own region counts 1.5×.
- **Evaluate builds.** The dashboard button (or `npm run eval:builds`) tests the draft-adjusted builds against today's lane builds on your stored games, with no player in both training and test. If they win with 95 % confidence, they become the default. You can always switch by hand.
- **Your games.**
  - Your item timings against the high-elo median, and which options work for you.
  - A post-game review: what the app showed, what you built, and your gold and CS against your lane opponent at 10 and 15 minutes.
- **In game, in Draft-adjusted mode.** The overlay and the Live Game card show the next item, re-ranked from the scoreboard (for example "adjusted for: fed Syndra (AP)"). It only switches after another item leads for three polls in a row. It is build advice only: no win percentage, no calls, no timers.
- **Smarter live counters.** Enemy damage type, healers and tanks come from what each champion does in your stored games, not from a fixed list.

## After installing
1. **⚙ Settings → Riot API key**: paste a fresh key if yours is older than 24 h, then **Match Database → ▶ Start fetching**.
2. **Draft dashboard → Evaluate builds** (about a minute). The report says whether the draft-adjusted builds beat the lane builds on your games. Run it again as more games arrive.
3. Play with the dashboard open: what it shows in champ select is linked to your game afterwards, for the review.
